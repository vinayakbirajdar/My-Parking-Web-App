Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a parking spot?

Select your vehicle type from the dashboard, choose your preferred location and time slot, then confirm your booking. You'll receive a confirmation with parking details.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept UPI, credit/debit cards, and wallet payments. You can also add money to your wallet for faster checkout.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes, you can cancel your booking up to 30 minutes before the scheduled time. Refunds will be processed to your original payment method within 3-5 business days.

How do I add a new vehicle?

Go to your Profile section, click on "Vehicle Information", and select "Add Vehicle". Enter your vehicle details and registration number.

What if I can't find my parking spot?

Check your booking confirmation for the exact location and spot number. If you're still having trouble, contact our support team for immediate assistance.

Common Issues

App Not Loading

Try clearing your app cache or restarting the application. Ensure you have a stable internet connection.

⚠️
Payment Failed

Check your payment method details and ensure sufficient balance. Contact your bank if the issue persists.

⚠️
